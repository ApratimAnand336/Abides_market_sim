PART 0. WHY THIS AMENDMENT (findings of N4, verified independently 2026-10-13)
=========================================================================================
0.1 Round 1 (16 designs x 5 seeds) fixed T4 (vv_corr +0.32..+0.66), T5 (volume after a
    jump x2.3..5.3) and T7 (informed agents profit) in every design. With fast, precise
    research T1/T2 pass (ac1 ~0, VR(5) ~1.2). No design passed all limits, so the v1.4
    selection rule named no winner and round 2 could not start.

0.2 Root cause of the remaining failures (T3, T6, the long-horizon lag). Re-run of
    D-60fl2M, seed 42:
      price vol / value vol by horizon: 1 min 0.49, 5 min 0.57, 15 min 0.73, 30 min 0.85,
      60 min 0.93  -> the price follows value, but with a lag of tens of minutes.
      +2% value jump at 11:00: informed agents whose belief is within $2 of the new value:
      0/60 at +2 min, 9/60 at +5, 36/60 at +8, 47/60 at +10, 59/60 at +15. The price stayed
      at $1001 for 2 minutes, then moved in steps as individual research readings arrived;
      the book split into an "old belief" and a "new belief" cluster with a hole between
      them (spread 124 ticks at +3 min, 141 at +8).
    The informed agents' price distrust rho in [3, 100] makes an agent that has not yet
    researched ignore the market even when the price runs $8 above its belief. Real
    traders without their own information learn from prices (Grossman-Stiglitz); that is
    how information spreads within minutes. This amendment adds that missing mechanism.

0.3 Two limits of spec v1.4 were mis-specified in absolute ticks (an error of the spec,
    not of the results). For a $1000 stock 1 tick = 0.1 bp. Real liquid large caps trade
    at about 1 bp (AAPL, MSFT), the 10 largest S&P 500 stocks about 2.5 bps, the index
    about 4.5 bps (Nasdaq, "Sampling the S&P 500 to minimize spreads",
    https://www.nasdaq.com/articles/sampling-sp-500-minimize-spreads). "Median <= 2 ticks"
    demanded 0.2 bp, tighter than AAPL. The v1.3 band G8 had the same unit error. These
    corrections rest on that external reference, not on the round-1 numbers; record them
    in DECISIONS.md as spec errors.

PART 2. THE MECHANISM: LEARNING FROM PRICES (fundamental.py, informed mode only)
=========================================================================================
2.1 Plain language (for AGENT_FUNDAMENTALIST.md)
  An informed agent normally trusts its own research much more than the market price.
  But if the price keeps sitting far from its belief - farther than normal price noise
  can explain, for several looks in a row - the agent concludes that other traders
  probably know something it does not, and it starts trusting the price more until the
  disagreement is gone. One odd print does nothing; a persistent move does. Research
  keeps every agent anchored to the true value, so prices cannot drift away for long.

2.2 Maths (Step 3 of v1.4 §4.3, market reading; only when price_learning is not "off")
  Before the market update, with x_hat and P after Steps 1-2 (state: eps_hist = the agent's
  last n_pl values of eps, epsbar = 0 at start):
      y       = mid - x_hat                                 (innovation, cents)
      eps     = y^2 / (P + R_noise)                         (surprise against normal price
                                                              noise; NOT against R_m)
      append eps to eps_hist (keep the last n_pl)
      e_pers  = min(eps_hist) if len(eps_hist) == n_pl else 0
                                                            (persistence gate: the price must
                                                             disagree on n_pl looks IN A ROW)
      epsbar  = (1 - a_pl) * epsbar + a_pl * e_pers
      g       = clip(epsbar / eps_thr, 1, rho)              (trust boost, at most rho)
      if g > 1:  P = max(P, (epsbar - eps_thr) * (P + R_noise))
                                                            (covariance matching: a persistent
                                                             disagreement means my own belief
                                                             is less certain than P says)
      rho_eff = rho / g                                     (>= 1)
      R_m     = rho_eff * R_noise                           (replaces rho * R_noise)
  then the v1.4 update K_m = P / (P + R_m), x_hat += K_m * y, P = (1 - K_m) P.
  Log in EKF_UPDATE: "eps", "e_pers", "epsbar", "g", "rho_eff", "P_inflated" (bool).
  Both parts are needed (checked by hand simulation, 2026-10-13, one agent, 60 s looks,
  R_noise = 45,000 c^2, P = 900 c^2, a persistent 2,000 c gap): lowering R_m alone moves
  the belief only 16-37% of the gap after 12 looks, because P is small; adding the P
  inflation adopts 59-96% on the first gated look and >= 95% one look later.
  Why the gate: without it a single large noise order would shift every agent's belief.
  With n_pl = 3 a two-look blip of 800 c moved beliefs by at most 14 c; normal price noise
  (gap sd 40 c, 3,000 looks) never triggered g > 1.
  Standard technique: innovation-based adaptive Kalman filtering (covariance matching);
  economically, learning from prices (Grossman-Stiglitz).

2.3 Parameters (param_ranges.py: PRICE_LEARNING_LEVELS; eps_thr and a_pl drawn per agent
    AFTER all existing informed draws of that agent; nothing is drawn for "off")
  level      n_pl (fixed)   eps_thr (log-U)   a_pl (U)
  "off"      (no learning; exactly the v1.4 behaviour, no draws)
  "mod"      3              [1.0, 4.0]        [0.2, 0.4]
  "strong"   2              [0.5, 2.0]        [0.2, 0.4]
  "strong" reacts one look earlier and also follows a two-look blip (hand simulation: an 800 c
  two-look blip moved beliefs by 35 c to $6.31): it is the herding-prone end of the sweep, which L6 and the
  selection rule judge.
  build_config: informed_design gains optional key "price_learning": "off" | "mod" |
  "strong" (absent = "off").

2.4 Tests (abides-markets/tests/test_fundamental_informed.py)
  T-N16 "off" is bit-identical to v1.4 informed mode: a 1-hour market with informed agents
        built without the key and with "price_learning": "off" gives the same EXEC hash
        and the same agent hyperparameters.
  T-N17 maths: a hand-built sequence of mids against a fixed starting belief: eps, e_pers,
        epsbar, g, the inflated P, rho_eff and K_m match 2.2 to 1e-9 at every step;
        g <= rho and rho_eff >= 1 always.
  T-N18 behaviour (one agent, belief 0, P = 900, R_noise = 45,000, q*sigma_f2*dt = 180,
        rho in {3, 100}, eps_thr at both ends of its range, a_pl = 0.3):
        "mod": a persistent 2,000 c gap is >= 50% adopted by the 3rd look and >= 90% by the
        4th; a two-look 800 c blip followed by the belief price moves the belief < 20 c.
        "strong": a persistent gap is >= 70% adopted by the 2nd look.
        Both: gaps drawn N(0, 40 c) for 3,000 looks never give g > 1.
  T-N19 I1-I9 and I8 (v1.3 default market) still pass.
