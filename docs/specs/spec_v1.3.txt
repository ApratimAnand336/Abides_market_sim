PART 0. CONTEXT
=========================================================================
0.1 Project
  FinVerse (startup + IIT Bombay ITSP 2026 project) is a realistic simulated stock
  market where students trade against AI investors and learn the theory alongside.
  The engine is a fork of ABIDES (J.P. Morgan's discrete-event market simulator).
  Repository: ApratimAnand336/Abides_market_sim. Latest branch: v1.2 (aa5f606).
  Prices are integer CENTS internally; variances are cents^2; engine time is ns.
  Price is about $1000 (r_bar = 100,000 cents). 1 tick = 1 cent.

  Read first: docs/engine/README.md (index), ARCHITECTURE.md, ORACLE.md,
  AGENTS_ABIDES.md, AGENT_FUNDAMENTALIST.md, AGENT_MOMENTUM.md, KNOWN_ISSUES.md,
  TESTING.md, and docs/v1.2/REPORT.md.

0.2 The market we measure (rmsc04_ekf.build_config, called through
    api.jobs.build_run_config)
  1000 NoiseAgent, 97 ValueAgent, 2 AdaptiveMarketMakerAgent (cold start, spread cap
  100 c), 20 FundamentalistAgent and 12 MomentumEKFAgent (ours; the "20 + 12" setup of
  every acceptance run), 1 ExchangeAgent, 1 NewsOracleAgent. Full day 09:30-16:00.
  True value: PregeneratedOracle, OU on a 1 s grid, generated once per seed before any
  agent, identical across agent mixes, news and session length (v1.2).

0.3 Where we stand (v1.2 acceptance, 2026-10-09; single runs A/B/C = seeds 42/42/7)
  Works: our agents track the price, trade moderately, react to news sensibly; the
  true value is seed-stable; the market starts without value agents; invariants I1-I7
  guard determinism, conservation and book sanity.
  Not realistic (KNOWN_ISSUES K1-K12):
      1-min return std        0.62-0.70 bps            (a large-cap: ~5-10 bps)
      lag-1 autocorrelation   +0.33 to +0.40           (real: ~0)
      |r| lag-1 autocorr      0.05-0.20                (real: clearly positive)
      excess kurtosis         1.8-3.2
      gap std / half-life     $0.13-0.14 / 1.3-1.4 min (price pinned to value)
      regime contrast         2-3x (spec v1.2 App. B; not re-measured in v1.2)
      price impact            never measured
  All of these are single-seed numbers. The kurtosis expectation moved from 6.7-8.0 to
  1.8-3.2 between two true-value paths of the same seed, so single runs are not
  evidence. R2 replaces them with 20-seed distributions.

0.4 Measured on 2026-10-11 (seed 42, full day, 20 + 12, v1.2 code; independent run):
      shares executed in the day (each trade counted once):   608,106
      share of executed volume by class (both sides counted, sums to 100%):
          ValueAgent 87.58%, NoiseAgent 6.85%, AdaptiveMarketMaker 4.75%,
          FundamentalistAgent 0.42%, MomentumEKFAgent 0.41%
      the one heavy fundamentalist (id 1109): 1,433 shares, 0.12% of volume.
      Its draw is the aggressive corner of most ranges (delta 0.0009, lambda_er 0.59,
      a_max 0.99, z0 1.48, b_f 0.012, k 2.49). It is a personality, not a bug.
  These numbers set the order sizes of the impact test (Part 5.4) and show that R2's
  market-level statistics are driven by the value agents.

0.5 A diagnostic observation that R2 must confirm or reject (back-of-envelope, not a
    measurement): the true value's 1-min change std is about 11-12 c (ORACLE.md; about
    1.1-1.2 bps of $1000), but the PRICE's 1-min return std is only 0.62-0.70 bps. The
    price looks SMOOTHER than the value it follows. A price that trails a moving target
    step by step has positive return autocorrelation and lower short-horizon volatility,
    which is exactly what we see. If R2 confirms it (price/value volatility ratio < 1,
    variance ratio VR(5) > 1), then even perfect tracking of today's value would give
    only ~1.2 bps: the 5-10 bps target needs a more volatile value process AND
    endogenous volatility, not only looser tracking. This is the central hypothesis R2
    tests; it decides how R3 and R4 are designed.

0.6 Roadmap position

PART 3. METRICS (exact definitions; single source: api/realism.py)
=========================================================================
All metrics are pure functions of numpy arrays in a new module api/realism.py, reused
by the lab (Part 7) and the dashboard (Part 9). Reuse api/stats.py where it already
computes the same thing (acf, returns_1m, gap_half_life); do not change those
functions' current outputs (the dashboard depends on them). Add new functions instead.

Common inputs, per run (built by one helper, realism.series_from_end_state or from an
existing payload's full-resolution arrays before downsampling):
  grid      1 s grid over the session, t = 0 .. duration (s since 09:30)
  mid_1s    L1 mid in dollars on the grid (last value at or before t; forward-filled)
  val_1s    true value in dollars on the grid (oracle.value_at / 100, exact float)
  spr_1s    L1 spread in cents on the grid (forward-filled; one-sided book -> NaN,
            excluded from spread statistics, counted in a "one_sided_share" field)
  close_1m  mid at the end of each 1-min bar (390 bars for a full day)
  r1        1-min log returns of close_1m (389 values for a full day)
  vol_1m    shares executed per 1-min bar (each EXEC counted once)
  execs     list of (t, price, qty, agent_id, oppos_agent_id)

3.1 Volatility
  sigma_1m_bps  = 1e4 * std(r1, ddof=1)
  sigma_day_implied = sigma_1m_bps * sqrt(389) / 100        (percent per day)
  day_return    = ln(mid at 16:00 / mid at 09:30)
  Across seeds (lab only): sigma_day_xseed = std over seeds of day_return (percent).
  Derivation for the band: volatility scales like sqrt(time); 1% a day over 389
  1-min returns is 1% / sqrt(389) = 5.07 bps per minute; 2% is 10.1 bps.

3.2 Predictability
  ac1          = lag-1 autocorrelation of r1 (api.stats.acf)
  ac_2_5       = autocorrelations at lags 2..5, and n_out = how many of lags 1..5 lie
                 outside +/- 2/sqrt(n) (= +/-0.101 for n = 389)
  VR(k)        = Var(ln mid(t+k min) - ln mid(t)) / (k * Var(r1)), overlapping k-min
                 returns from close_1m, k in {5, 30}. Random walk: VR = 1. Positive
                 autocorrelation (price trailing a target): VR > 1.

3.3 Volatility clustering
  acf_abs      = autocorrelation of |r1| at lags 1..30 (api.stats.acf with n_lags = 30)
  CS           = mean(acf_abs[1..5])     (clustering strength)
  PS           = mean(acf_abs[10..30])   (persistence)

3.4 Fat tails
  kurt         = excess kurtosis of r1 (Fisher; scipy.stats.kurtosis)
  skew         = skewness of r1 (reported only)

3.5 Volume and volatility
  5-min buckets b = 1..78: RV_b = sqrt(sum of r1^2 in the bucket), V_b = shares
  executed in the bucket.
  vv_corr      = Spearman rank correlation of (RV_b, V_b)
  u_ratio      = (volume 09:30-10:00 + volume 15:30-16:00) / (2 * mean 30-min volume
                 over 10:00-15:30)    (intraday U-shape, K7; report only)

3.6 Spread
  spread_med_ticks, spread_p95_ticks, spread_max_ticks from spr_1s (two-sided only);
  one_sided_share = share of grid seconds with an empty side.

3.7 Price vs true value
  gap = mid_1s - val_1s (dollars).
  gap_std, gap_mean_abs, corr_mid_value (Pearson of mid_1s and val_1s).
  gap_half_life_min = api.stats.gap_half_life(gap, lags_s = LAB_GAP_LAGS) with
      LAB_GAP_LAGS = (60, 120, 300, 600, 1200, 1800, 3600)
  (The dashboard keeps its own (60, 120, 300, 600). The lab needs the longer lags
  because the target band is 30-90 min: at a 90-min half-life rho(600 s) = 0.926 and
  rho(60 s) = 0.992, which the 0.98 cut excludes. Record both lag sets' results.)
  vol_ratio_price_value = std(1-min log returns of mid) / std(1-min log returns of the
      true value sampled at the same bar ends)        (the Part 0.5 hypothesis)

3.8 Regimes
  regime_contrast = max_w RV_w / min_w RV_w over the 13 non-overlapping 30-min windows
      of the day, RV_w = std of the 1-min returns inside window w.
      Note for the report: with constant volatility and 29 returns per window, sampling
      noise alone gives a ratio of about 1.55 (10th-90th percentile 1.38-1.84;
      simulated 2026-10-11, 500 normal days), so 2-3x means "no regimes".
  trend_share = share of 1-min-stepped rolling 30-min windows whose |30-min return|
      exceeds 2x the median |30-min return| of the day (report only).

3.9 Accounting and agent sanity (per run; P&L at the closing mid)
  pnl_vs_hold per agent (api.export.pnl_vs_hold) and per class: total, median, p10, p90.
  zero_sum = sum of pnl_vs_hold over EVERY agent (including the probe of Part 5.4) in
      cents. Must be exactly 0. A non-zero value is a hard failure of the run.
  per class: orders sent, shares traded (both sides), volume share (rows sum to 100),
      and for our agents turnover per agent (median, p90, max) and holdings p10-p90
      (sampled every 10 s from HOLDINGS_UPDATED, pooled).

3.10 Heavy-trader watch (D-R2-4)
  top_fund_volume_share = largest single fundamentalist's share of executed volume (%).
  top_fund_turnover     = its shares traded; and its drawn parameters.
  Report its distribution over seeds. No band; flag in the report if any seed exceeds
  1% of total volume.

PART 6. SCENARIO TOOLING (new code; every default is inert, Part 2)
=========================================================================
6.1 Oracle path source "scenario" (abides-markets/abides_markets/oracles/
    pregenerated_oracle.py)
  Register PATH_SOURCES["scenario"] = generate_scenario_ou. It takes the same params as
  synthetic_ou plus params["events"], a list of dicts:
      {"type": "jump", "t_ns": int, "cents": float}           value step
      {"type": "vol",  "t0_ns": int, "t1_ns": int, "mult": float}  innovation scaling
  Algorithm (no extra random draws, same draw order as synthetic_ou):
    1. Draw exactly what generate_synthetic_ou draws, in the same order (refactor the
       shared part into one helper so the two cannot diverge).
    2. innov[1:] = sd * eps; for each vol event, multiply innov[k] by mult for every k
       with t0_ns <= times[k] < t1_ns.
    3. Add megashocks exactly as now.
    4. For each jump event: innov[k] += cents at the first grid index k with
       times[k] >= t_ns.
    5. x = lfilter([1], [1, -a], innov); return r_bar + x.
  Consequences: with events = [] the path is bit-identical to synthetic_ou (T-L1); a
  jump of J adds J * a^(k - k0) at k >= k0 (a = exp(-kappa*1 s); over 5 hours the jump
  decays by less than 0.0003%, so it is permanent in practice).
  The oracle keeps self.events = the list as applied (with the grid time actually used)
  and logs it; api/export adds payload.oracle_events (schema change, Part 9).
  build_config gains oracle_events=None (list of dicts with offsets as "HH:MM:SS" and
  "pct" of r_bar for jumps; converted to the ns/cents form above). None -> path_source
  stays "synthetic_ou" (the default path is untouched).

6.2 Probe agent (new file abides-markets/abides_markets/agents/impact_probe_agent.py)
  class ImpactProbeAgent(TradingAgent): a test instrument, not a market participant.
  Constructor: symbol, side ("BUY"/"SELL"), quantity, start_time (ns), duration_s=300,
      child_interval_s=10, starting_cash, starting_shares, random_state.
  Behaviour: if quantity == 0 it never requests a wakeup beyond what TradingAgent
      requires and never sends an order. Otherwise at start_time + i*child_interval
      (i = 0..n-1) it places a MARKET order (place_market_order) for the child size.
      It logs PROBE_CHILD (i, planned qty, time) and ORDER_EXECUTED as usual, and at
      kernel stop PROBE_SUMMARY (planned, filled, unfilled, vwap_cents, arrival_mid).
      It uses no random numbers.
  Seeding and placement (to keep every other agent's RNG identical):
      appended as the LAST agent in build_config, after the NewsOracleAgent and BEFORE
      the kernel random state and the latency model are drawn;
      its random_state = np.random.RandomState(0), NOT drawn from the global np.random.
      generate_latency_model draws one uniform coordinate per agent in agent order, so
      the first n coordinates (all existing agents) are unchanged by one more agent.
  build_config gains probe=None (dict: side, quantity, start "HH:MM:SS", duration_s,
      child_interval_s). None -> no probe agent (default unchanged).
  The probe is excluded from the population table's classes but listed separately
  ("Impact probe (test)") and included in zero_sum.

6.3 Market-maker pause windows (adaptive_market_maker_agent.py)
  New constructor parameter pause_windows: Optional[List[Tuple[int, int]]] = None
  (absolute ns, [start, end)). Default None: no code path changes (I8).
  If given: kernel_starting schedules one extra wakeup at each window start; at a wake
  inside a window the agent calls cancel_all_orders(), logs MM_PAUSED once per window,
  does not request the spread, and schedules its next wake as usual; at its first wake
  at or after the window end it resumes normally and logs MM_RESUMED.
  build_config gains mm_pause_windows=None (list of ("HH:MM:SS", "HH:MM:SS") pairs),
  passed to both market makers.

6.4 Momentum capital multiplier (rmsc04_ekf.py)
  build_config gains mom_capital_mult=1.0. Our MomentumEKFAgents get
  starting_cash = starting_cash * mom_capital_mult (the endowment rule then gives
  floor(0.5 * cash / r_bar) shares). At 1.0 nothing changes (I8). No random draws.

6.5 Lab entry point for configs
  New module api/lab.py: lab_config(config_id, seed) -> the build_config(...) dict for
  one entry of the run matrix (Appendix C), built through the same arguments as
  api.jobs.build_run_config plus the new keyword arguments. RunParams and the dashboard
  sidebar do NOT gain these switches in R2 (scenario controls for students are later
  product work).

PART 7. THE LAB RUNNER (scripts/realism_lab.py)

7.1 CLI
    python scripts/realism_lab.py --campaign c1|c2|c3|c4|all|regression
                                   [--seeds 42,7,...] [--workers N] [--force]
                                   [--configs C-DEF,S2,...] [--report-only]

  Default workers = min(os.cpu_count() - 1, 6). One simulation per process

  Resumable: a run whose result exists with the same engine_version and config hash is

7.2 Per-run outputs (runs/_lab/<config_id>/<seed>/; runs/ is already git-ignored)
  metrics.json  every Part 3 metric, the config dict (minus objects), seed,
                engine_version (api.jobs.engine_version()), config sha256, wall time,
                zero_sum, invariant flags, oracle events applied, probe summary.
  series.npz    float32 arrays: mid_1s, val_1s, spr_1s, close_1m, vol_1m, and for our
                agents per-agent x_hat (fundamentalists) and MOM_STATE transitions
                (momentum) on a 10 s grid; per-class signed net shares per 1-min bar.
                (~0.3 MB per run; needed for the treated - baseline comparisons.)
  Do NOT store full payloads (8 MB each).

7.3 Scenario analysis (api/realism.py, functions taking treated and baseline series)
  All differences are treated minus baseline on the same seed, on the 1 s grid,
  smoothed with a centred 30 s rolling mean where stated. Times in minutes after t0.

  Value-jump scenarios (S2, S2n, S3, S7c); J = signed jump in dollars:
    f(t)          = (mid_T(t) - mid_B(t)) / J              (share of the jump priced in)
    t50, t90      = first t >= t0 where smoothed f >= 0.5 / 0.9 (None if not within
                    90 min)
    overshoot     = max(0, max over [t0, t0+60] of smoothed f - 1)   (fraction of J)
    settle        = mean f over [t0+60, t0+90]
    belief_5, belief_30 = median over fundamentalists of (x_hat_T - x_hat_B)/J at
                    t0+5 and t0+30 min
    mom_entries   = entries (BREAKOUT + REENTRY) in the jump's direction in
                    [t0, t0+30], treated minus baseline, summed over our momentum agents
    net_flow[c]   = net shares bought by class c in [t0, t0+60], treated minus baseline
                    (who bought the move, who sold it)
    vol_uplift    = sigma_1m in [t0, t0+30] treated / baseline
    volume_uplift = shares executed in [t0, t0+30] treated / baseline
  News-only scenarios (S4, S4n); s = sign of the sentiment:
    R(t)          = s * 1e4 * ln(mid_T(t) / mid_B(t))      (bps, positive = expected
                    direction)
    R_peak, t_peak = max of smoothed R over [t0, t0+30] and its time
    t_half_rev    = first t > t_peak where smoothed R <= R_peak / 2 (None within 90 min)
    R_60          = mean R over [t0+55, t0+65]
    belief_shift  = median fundamentalist NEWS_RECEIVED shift ($), median momentum
                    change in v_hat
  Volatility burst (S5):
    in_ratio      = sigma_1m over [11:00, 12:00] treated / baseline
    after_ratio   = sigma_1m over [12:00, 13:00] treated / baseline  (clustering: > 1.2
                    would mean volatility persists after its cause ends)
    spread_ratio, volume_ratio, gap_std in [11:00, 12:00] treated / baseline
  Market-maker pause (S6), window W = [13:00, 13:30]:
    spread mean, p95, max and one_sided_share in W, treated vs baseline;
    sigma_1m in W treated / baseline; shares executed in W treated / baseline;
    max |mid_T - mid_B| in W ($); recovery_min = minutes after 13:30 until the 5-min
    rolling mean spread first falls to <= 1.2x the baseline's over the same 5 min.
  Momentum capital (S7a, S7b): scorecard deltas (G1-G11 metrics) vs C-DEF on the same
    seed; momentum volume share; entries per agent per day; momentum P&L vs hold.
  Price impact (IMP-*), s = +1 for BUY, -1 for SELL:
    I(t)          = s * 1e4 * ln(mid_T(t) / mid_B(t))      (bps)
    I_end         = mean I over [t0+D-10 s, t0+D]          (end of execution)
    I_peak        = max over [t0, t0+D+120 s] of the 30 s rolling mean of I
    I_perm        = mean I over [t0+D+25 min, t0+D+35 min]
    shortfall     = s * 1e4 * (VWAP of the probe's fills / arrival mid - 1)  (bps; what
                    the trader paid), arrival mid = mid_B(t0) (= mid_T(t0))
    fill_rate     = filled / Q
    absorbed[c]   = shares the probe traded against each class (who was on the other
                    side)
    Noise floor   = p90 over seeds of |I_end| and |I_perm| for IMP-0. A size whose
                    median |I_end| is below 2x the floor is reported as "not resolved".
    Exponent beta = OLS slope of ln(median I_end) on ln(median q) over the four BUY
                    sizes; 90% CI by bootstrap over seeds (2000 resamples, fixed RNG
                    seed 0 for the bootstrap). Graded as G12. Also report beta for
                    I_peak.
    Recovery      = median over seeds of I_perm / I_peak for IMP-1 and IMP-5; graded as
                    G13 on IMP-1.
    Side asymmetry = IMP-1s versus IMP-1 (I_end, I_perm, shortfall).

APPENDIX A. WHY THESE MEASURES (short theory)
=========================================================================
A1 Volatility scales with sqrt(time) for a random walk: sigma_k = sigma_1 * sqrt(k).
   Hence 1-2% a day = 5.1-10.1 bps per minute over 389 one-minute returns.
A2 Under the null of no predictability, sample autocorrelations of n returns have a
   standard error of about 1/sqrt(n): with n = 389 the 95% band is +/-0.101.
A3 Variance ratio (Lo-MacKinlay): VR(k) = 1 + 2 * sum_{j=1}^{k-1} (1 - j/k) rho_j.
   A price that trails a smoothly moving target has rho_j > 0 for small j, so VR > 1
   and short-horizon volatility is understated relative to long-horizon volatility.
A4 Volatility clustering shows as positive autocorrelation of |r| decaying slowly
   (stylized fact; R. Cont, Quantitative Finance, 2001). A constant-volatility process
   has acf(|r|) ~ 0.
A5 Gap half-life: treat the mispricing as a spring, dg = -k g dt + noise, so
   rho(L) = exp(-k L) and t_1/2 = ln 2 / k (spec v1.1 Appendix A5).
A6 Square-root impact law: the price impact of a metaorder of size Q executed over a
   short time scales roughly like sigma_day * sqrt(Q / V_day) across many markets
   (e.g. Toth et al., Physical Review X, 2011; Bouchaud et al., "Trades, Quotes and
   Prices", 2018). Impact then partly decays after the execution ends. beta is the
   measured exponent; 0.5 is the reference.
A7 Implementation shortfall (Perold, 1988): the cost of executing relative to the price
   when the decision was made. For a student, this is what "my order moved the price
   against me" costs in bps.
A8 Why treated minus baseline on the same seed: the true value, every agent's
   parameters and every RNG stream are identical until the trigger, so any later
   difference is caused by the trigger and the market's response to it. Divergence
   grows after the trigger (agents' random draws are consumed differently once their
   decisions differ); the placebo IMP-0 measures that divergence, which is why
   impact numbers are judged against its noise floor.
