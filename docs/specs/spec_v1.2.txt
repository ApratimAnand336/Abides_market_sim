PART 0. CONTEXT
====================================================================
0.1 Project
  FinVerse (startup + IIT Bombay ITSP 2026 project) is a realistic simulated stock market where
  students trade against AI investors and learn the theory alongside. The engine is a fork of
  ABIDES (J.P. Morgan's discrete-event market simulator). Repository:
  ApratimAnand336/Abides_market_sim.
  Branches: Momentum (MomentumEKFAgent), dashboard (FastAPI api/ + React web/),
            v1.1 (spec v1.1, all done).
  Prices are integer CENTS internally; variances are in cents^2. Price is about $1000
  (r_bar = 100,000 cents).

0.2 Market composition (rmsc04_ekf.py defaults; the dashboard can change counts)
  1000 NoiseAgent, 97 ValueAgent (stock ABIDES), 2 AdaptiveMarketMakerAgent,
  up to 20 FundamentalistAgent (ours, Kalman filter on the market price),
  up to 12 MomentumEKFAgent (ours, 2-state Kalman trend filter),
  1 ExchangeAgent, 1 NewsOracleAgent. Every agent starts with $100,000. Our agents hold 50% of
  that in stock at the open (50 shares).

0.3 What v1.1 achieved (verified independently 2026-10-08, seed 42, full day)
  Fundamentalist: no drift (beliefs within about $0.13 of the market), the gain stays about
  0.025 all day, no dumping of the endowment; news shifts are $0.28-1.55 instead of $33.
  Momentum: all 12 agents can enter trends; they see 60-83% of the true trend speed; position
  scale about 0.9 in trends. Dashboard D1-D5 are in. 55 agent tests and 22 export tests pass.

0.4 Problems found after v1.1 that this spec fixes (evidence from experiments on 2026-10-09)
  P1 Fundamentalist overtrading: each traded a median of about 1,900 shares/day on a 50-share
     endowment, swinging 0 <-> 100 shares, with all 20 in sync. Together they placed more
     orders than all 97 value agents. Cause: z = gap/sqrt(P) compares the gap only with the
     agent's own belief uncertainty (about $0.30), not with the normal noise of the price
     (sqrt(R) about $2). Tested fix z = gap/sqrt(P+R): turnover 168-231 shares/day, holdings
     46-57 (10th-90th percentile), loss vs holding $5-13 instead of $80-110; market kurtosis
     2.5-3.0 -> 6.7-8.0 (more realistic).
  P2 The true value is not reproducible: SparseMeanRevertingOracle generates the fundamental
     LAZILY. Each query draws one random number from the oracle's RNG, conditioned on the time
     since the last query. The realised path therefore depends on how many queries happen and
     when. Same seed with vs without news: identical for 10 min, $0.10 apart at 30 min, about
     $2 apart after 2 h. Megashock times also use numpy's GLOBAL RNG. Consequence: no clean
     counterfactuals, and in future a user's trade would change the company's true value.
  P3 Market-maker death: AdaptiveMarketMakerAgent (polling mode), on a wake where the book has
     no bid or ask and it has no last mid, places nothing AND never calls set_wakeup again.
     With 3 value agents both MMs were dead all day (0 orders) and spreads reached $1.37.
  P4 Market cannot start without value agents: with num_value = 0 there are zero orders all
     day. Value agents are the only agents that place an order into an empty book; everyone
     else waits for a two-sided book. (P3 makes this permanent.)
  P5 No automated guarantees (determinism, conservation of cash and shares, book sanity).

0.5 Explicitly NOT in this spec (planned later, do not implement)
  R2 realism lab (stylized-facts scorecard, scenarios); R3 fundamentalist "research channel"
  and step-by-step replacement of ValueAgents; R4 information news moving value,
  calm/stressed volatility periods, MM inventory limits/skew; R5 calibration; R6 user
  trading. The skewed initial noise, the uncertainty-based safety margin and the herding
  tilt stay deferred.

1.1 Text to adapt for ORACLE.md, "What the oracle is (and is not)"
  - Two different things exist in the simulator:
      the MARKET PRICE: produced by agents' orders meeting in the order book. Market
        dynamics (volatility, trends, crashes, clustering) must come from here.
      the TRUE VALUE ("fundamental"): what the company is really worth. In real life nobody
        observes it directly; it changes when information arrives (earnings, news).
        Informed traders estimate it with noise.
  - The oracle is the simulator's source of the true value. It is NOT historical market data.
    ABIDES' SparseMeanRevertingOracle invents the value with a random process: an
    Ornstein-Uhlenbeck random walk with very weak pull to r_bar, plus rare "megashocks".
    Its docstring says: "Historical dates are effectively meaningless to this oracle. It is
    driven by the numpy random number seed."
  - Why synthetic and not historical: a historical price series is itself a market price
    (made by real traders), not a true value. Using it as the fundamental would make our
    agents replay history instead of producing their own dynamics. Synthetic value lets us
    control the information environment (calm days, shocks, news) and measure how the
    market reacts.
  - Measured on seed 42: value changes about 1.6-1.9 cents per sqrt(second); a whole day
    spans about $2-4. The mean-reversion pull (kappa = 1.67e-16 per ns) is negligible within
    a day. Megashocks (rate 2.78e-18 per ns, a mean gap of about 11 years; size about +/-$10)
    essentially never happen within a session. That calmness is one root cause of the market's low volatility, to be
    addressed in R4 (calm/stressed periods, information jumps).
  - Historical data still has a place later: as an optional path source ("replay the
    true-value shape of a real stock or event"), which the v1.2 design makes a drop-in.

1.2 Text to adapt for AGENTS_ABIDES.md, "Why the market currently depends on ValueAgents"
  ValueAgents do three jobs at once:
    (1) INFORMATION: they observe the true value with small noise (variance r_bar/100 =
        1000 cents^2, about 32 cents std) about every 175 s, so they pull the price toward
        value and are the only consistently profitable group;
    (2) LIQUIDITY and VOLUME: about 13,000 orders of about 100 shares per day, about 83% of
        traded volume;
    (3) STARTING THE MARKET: they are the only agents that will place an order into an empty
        book (at their value estimate).
  v1.2 removes dependency (3) for market makers (cold start, Part 4). R3 builds our
  fundamentalist to take over (1) and, with enough agents and capital, (2). After that the
  ValueAgents can be phased out. Until then they stay as scaffolding.

PART 2. FUNDAMENTALIST: SIGNAL-TO-NOISE SIZING (fixes P1)
====================================================================
File: abides-markets/abides_markets/agents/fundamental.py (target_position, execute_strategy).

2.1 Change
  Store the observation variance used in the current update: self.R_last = R
  (set in execute_strategy right after _compute_observation_variance).
  In target_position:
      z = (x_hat - mid) / sqrt(P + R_last)       (P = posterior variance after correction)
  Guard: if P + R_last <= 0, z = 0. The rest of target_position is unchanged:
      w_star = clip(w0 + a_max*C_t*tanh(z/z0), 0, 1), step cap s_max, dead band b_f, side
      from d.
  Parameter ranges unchanged (a_max 0.6-1.0, z0 1-3, s_max 0.10-0.25, b_f 0.01-0.03).

2.2 Meaning (put in AGENT_FUNDAMENTALIST.md)
  P + R is the variance of (belief - price) when nothing is mispriced: my own uncertainty
  plus the normal noise of the price. z is therefore a t-statistic, signal / noise:
  z = 1 is one normal wiggle; z = 3 is a real mispricing.
  Worked example (seed 42 medians): gap 13 cents, sqrt(P) = 30 cents, sqrt(R) = $2.09.
      old z = 0.13/0.30 = 0.43 -> about 9 shares of target change
      new z = 0.13/2.11 = 0.06 -> about 1.3 shares, below the dead band -> hold
  Side effect: R rises in choppy markets (low ER), so the agent trades less in chop and
  more when prices move cleanly.
  Why the old version herded: all agents watch the same price through similar lagging
  filters, so their gaps share sign and timing. Every dip looked like a strong buy signal
  to all of them at once.

2.3 Logging
  FUND_DECISION adds "z_den" (= sqrt(P + R_last), cents) and keeps "z".
  Export: no payload change is required. If the fundamental detail view shows z, label it
  "edge (signal/noise)".

2.4 Tests (update existing ones that assumed z = gap/sqrt(P))
  T-F8  z uses P + R_last: with P = 900, R_last = 40,000 and gap = 100 cents,
        z = 100/sqrt(40,900) (+/-1e-9).
  T-F9  Typical gap, no trade: gap = 13 cents, P = 900, R = 43,681, C = 0.5, a_max = 0.9,
        z0 = 2.1, W = $100k, holdings at 50% -> no order (dead band).
  T-F10 Large gap trades: gap = $6 under the same settings -> a BUY of >= 1 share and
        <= s_max*W/mid.

PART 3. PRE-GENERATED, SEED-STABLE ORACLE (fixes P2)
====================================================================
3.1 New class
  File: abides-markets/abides_markets/oracles/pregenerated_oracle.py
  class PregeneratedOracle(Oracle)   (keep SparseMeanRevertingOracle untouched for the stock
                                      ABIDES configs)
  Constructor: (mkt_open, mkt_close, symbols: Dict[str, Dict], grid_s: float = 1.0)
  Per symbol, a params dict like today's, plus:
      "path_source": "synthetic_ou"   (only this source in v1.2; design for more, see 3.4)
      "random_state": np.random.RandomState   (dedicated; the ONLY RNG the path may use)
  At construction, generate the whole path for [mkt_open, mkt_close] on a grid of grid_s
  seconds:
      t_k = mkt_open + k*grid_s*1e9,  k = 0..N   (N = ceil(duration/grid_s))
      v_0 = r_bar
      exact OU transition per step (same maths as compute_fundamental_at_timestamp):
          d = grid_s*1e9 ns;  a = exp(-kappa*d)
          v_{k+1} = r_bar + (v_k - r_bar)*a
                    + sqrt( fund_vol^2/(2*kappa) * (1 - a^2) ) * eps_k,   eps_k ~ N(0,1)
          Compute 1 - a^2 as -expm1(-2*kappa*d) for precision (kappa*d is about 1.7e-7
          here). If kappa*d < 1e-12, use the limit: the stdev is fund_vol*sqrt(d) and a = 1.
      megashocks: draw the arrival times as a Poisson process with rate megashock_lambda_a over
          the session, and the values from the same bimodal distribution as today
          (+/- megashock_mean with variance megashock_var, sign 50/50), ALL from the
          dedicated random_state. Add each shock at the first grid point >= its time
          (affecting all later points).
      store values as float; round to int cents only when served (as ABIDES does).
  Draw order must be fixed (the full eps vector first, then the megashocks) so the path is a
  pure function of (seed, params, grid, session).

3.2 Interface (drop-in for every current caller)
  observe_price(symbol, current_time, random_state, sigma_n=1000) -> int
      t < mkt_open  -> v_0;  t >= mkt_close -> last value;  otherwise the value at the
      last grid point <= t (a step function; document why: no look-ahead).
      sigma_n == 0 -> exact value; otherwise round(normal(value, sqrt(sigma_n))) drawn from
      the CALLER's random_state (unchanged semantics: sigma_n is a variance).
  get_daily_open_price(symbol, mkt_open, cents=True) -> v_0 (as int cents).
  f_log[symbol]: a list of {"FundamentalTime": t_k, "FundamentalValue": int(round(v_k))}
      for the whole grid, so ExchangeAgent and api/export keep working unchanged. If the
      export payload grows too much, downsample in export (LTTB already exists), not here.
  Also expose value_at(symbol, t) and path(symbol) -> (times_ns, values) for tests and future
  features.
  Any other method the current callers use (grep for "oracle." across abides-markets and
  api) must keep working.

3.3 Config
  rmsc04_ekf.py: build PregeneratedOracle instead of SparseMeanRevertingOracle, same
  parameters (kappa_oracle, fund_vol, megashock_*), grid_s = 1.0. The oracle's
  random_state seed is still drawn from the config's seeded np.random, as today. Remove
  every use of the global np.random from the oracle.
  NewsOracleAgent is a different thing (it broadcasts headlines). Do not rename it, but
  document in ARCHITECTURE.md that "news oracle" != "value oracle".

3.4 Designed for later (do not build now; leave clean extension points)
  path_source = "csv": read a (time, value) series and rescale it to r_bar (historical
                shape replay)
  path_source = "scenario": synthetic path plus scripted events (value jumps for
                information news, calm/stressed volatility periods), each event logged
  Implement path generation as a function generate_path(params, times, rng) so new sources
  are added by writing one function.

3.5 Tests
  T-O1 Same seed, different market: run (a) default, (b) the same plus one news event,

File: abides-markets/abides_markets/agents/market_makers/adaptive_market_maker_agent.py
4.1 New constructor parameter  cold_start_price: Optional[int] = None  (cents).
  Meaning: the price a market maker anchors to before any two-sided book exists, like
  "yesterday's close" which every real trader knows. It is used ONLY while the agent has
  neither a current mid nor a last mid.
4.2 Polling mode (subscribe = False), in receive_message, after both AWAITING_* flags are
    False:
      if mid is None: mid = self.last_mid
      if mid is None and self.cold_start_price is not None: mid = self.cold_start_price
      if mid is not None: self.place_orders(mid)
      ALWAYS: self.state = self.initialise_state(); self.set_wakeup(current_time +
              self.get_wake_frequency())
  (Today set_wakeup is reached only when mid is not None, which kills the agent forever.)
  Subscription mode: apply the same fallback for mid (no wake change is needed there).
  Log a "MM_COLD_START" event the first time cold_start_price is used.
4.3 Config: rmsc04_ekf.py passes cold_start_price = r_bar to both market makers.
4.4 Our agents: no change. With MMs quoting from the cold start, the book becomes two-sided
    at the open, so noise, fundamentalist and momentum agents can act.
4.5 Tests
  T-M6 Empty book: a market with num_value = 0 runs 1 hour; trades > 0; each MM places

  T-M7 Liveness: in the default config, each MM's number of wakes is >= 90% of

  Document in AGENTS_ABIDES.md: with num_mm = 0 and num_value = 0 the market still cannot

APPENDIX A. DECISION LOG SEED (for docs/engine/DECISIONS.md)
====================================================================
Use these entries as given (dates are when the owner approved). Add new decisions as they
are made.
  2026-10 (momentum spec v1.0)  MomentumEKFAgent: log-price damped-trend Kalman filter,
      warm-up fit, Kaufman-ER observation noise, regime monitor, state machine
      (NEUTRAL/BULL/BEAR/WATCH) with hysteresis, target-exposure sizing with a staircase
      ratchet, primed news handling, long-only, endowment 0.5. Reason: a trend follower that
      reasons about the speed and its uncertainty instead of fixed moving averages.
  2026-10 (v1.0)  Our agents start with 50% of wealth in stock (an endowment), so they can
      sell as well as buy. Reason: without shares, long-only fundamentalists could never act
      on "overvalued".
  2026-10-08 (v1.1)  Deleted the fundamentalist's +/-1% nudge after each order. Reason: it
      made a $10 jump per order, equivalent to a phantom price thousands of dollars away;
      beliefs drifted to about $949. Alternative (a herding tilt used only in decisions)
      deferred.
  2026-10-08 (v1.1)  Added process noise Q = q*sigma_f2_hat*dt (sigma_f2_hat estimated from
      300 s price changes, prior 3 cents^2/s). Reason: without Q the gain fell like 1/n and
      the belief froze into an all-day average. Constant Q rejected: it would not adapt to
      other oracle settings.
  2026-10-08 (v1.1)  Smaller observation noise delta in [0.0005, 0.003], lambda_er in
      [0.5, 2]. Reason: the old R said prices were $2.6-70 off when the real noise is cents
      to tens of cents. A self-calibrating R from innovations was deferred until the value
      agents are replaced.
  2026-10-08 (v1.1)  Target-position sizing (a holding target from the edge, a step cap, a
      dead band) replaced flow sizing. Reason: the flow rule plus a 1-share minimum sold the
      whole endowment within 7-19 minutes.
  2026-10-08 (v1.1)  News shift = sentiment*k_news*sqrt(sigma_f2_hat*1800 s), additive, with
      P += shift^2. Reason (owner's idea): scale news by how much the stock usually moves,
      not by a % of price; the added P makes the market confirm or fade the news. The spread
      was rejected as the scale (it measures liquidity, not movement).
  2026-10-08 (v1.1)  Momentum M1-M7: kappa_l [0.02, 0.15], kappa_v [1, 2]; detrended
      volatility; tau = tau_ratio*wake_mean with tau_ratio log-U[15, 40]; the ratchet cap
      follows down; re-entry needs T >= theta_out; a persistent stop exit. Reason: the filter
      saw only 26-28% of trend speed in the worst parameter corner; the trend counted as
      volatility; agents with tau/wake <= 5 could never enter; noisy re-entries.
  2026-10-08 (v1.1)  Dashboard D1-D5 (off-scale line breaks, P&L vs holding, overlay agent
      selection, market population panel with count controls, price vs true value panel).
  2026-10-08 (v1.1)  Deferred: skewed initial noise; self-calibrating R; the safety margin
      in uncertainty units; replacing value agents; two news types; the herding tilt.
  2026-10-09 (v1.2)  Fundamentalist edge z = gap/sqrt(P + R) (a t-statistic) instead of
      gap/sqrt(P). Reason: overtrading and synchronised herding; experiment evidence in
