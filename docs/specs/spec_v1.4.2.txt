PART 0. DECISIONS (owner, 2026-10-13)
=====================================================================================
D-R3-7 The selected design is D-60fl2M with price_learning "off": 60 informed
       fundamentalists, research interval log-U[60, 300] s, research noise log-U[10, 50] c,
       $2M each. It passes T1, T2, T4, T5, T7 and every limit (L1-L6, corrected units).
D-R3-8 R3 closes with T3 and T6 OPEN. They are carried into R4 as known issues, with the
       evidence below. No further R3 design sweeps.
       Evidence (RESULTS_s2.md): T3 price/value vol ratio 0.49 at 1 min, 0.73 at 15 min,
       0.93 at 60 min (smooth minute to minute, tracks value over the hour). T6 spread p95
       after a +/-2% jump 8.7x / 16.7x the baseline (book never empties; it widens while
       agents disagree; 33% of agents updated at +5 min, 87% at +10).
       Why R4: both need liquidity that stays tight while informed traders disagree, and
       short-horizon price variation that does not come from research arrivals. Those are
       R4's market-maker (inventory, quoting around the last trade under stress) and
       endogenous-volatility work.
D-R3-9 Learning from prices (v1.4.1 Part 2) is a documented negative result. The rule is
       symmetric: an agent that has just researched the new value sees a stale price and is
       pulled back to it (diagnostic: 2-3x more and larger moves toward the stale price).
       The spec's hand simulation tested only an un-updated agent; that was a spec error.
       Keep the code with default "off" (tests stay), document the result, and list a
       one-directional variant (no learning for an agent whose last research reading is
       younger than its research interval) as an R4 candidate. Do not implement it now.
D-R3-10 Speed matters for the rest of the roadmap (R4/R5 sweeps, R6 live users). Do the
       bit-identical engine speed-up of Part 1 before the remaining campaigns.
D-R3-11 The remaining campaigns are trimmed (Part 2). The final market keeps 20 seeds.

PART 1. ENGINE SPEED-UP (milestone N4d; ABIDES core, results must be bit-identical)
=====================================================================================
1.1 Problem: OrderBook.get_imbalance (abides-markets/abides_markets/order_book.py) is about
    40% of run time. It sums the visible quantity of every order on both sides of the book
    (PriceLevel.total_quantity iterates every order) and it is called by
    ExchangeAgent.handle_event_based_data_subscription for EVERY book event and EVERY
    BookImbalance subscriber (both market makers). Belief-anchored quotes spread orders over
    many price levels, so the book is large.
1.2 Change: keep running totals of visible quantity per side in OrderBook
    (self._vis_bid_qty, self._vis_ask_qty), updated at every place where a visible order
    enters, leaves, is partially executed, is modified or replaced (follow every code path
    that changes PriceLevel contents: insert, execute/fill, cancel, partial cancel, modify,
    replace; hidden orders do not count, as now). get_imbalance uses the totals; its
    formula and return values are unchanged. Optionally also memoise PriceLevel totals the
    same way. Do not change any other behaviour.
1.3 Guard (tests):
    T-S1 property test: in a 1-hour default market and in a 1-hour D-60fl2M market, after
         every book event the running totals equal the full recomputation (run with a debug
         flag that asserts on every call; the flag is off by default).
    T-S2 bit-identical results: I8 (v1.3 default market, unchanged hash) and the full-day
         seed-42 D-60fl2M-S2 EXEC hash recorded BEFORE the change (store it in
         docs/v1.4/lab/reference_hashes.json) are identical after the change.
    T-S3 report wall time of one full D-60fl2M day before/after (same machine, 1 worker).
    If T-S1 or T-S2 cannot be made to pass, revert the change and report; do not proceed
    with a non-identical engine.
