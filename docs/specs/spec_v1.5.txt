0.1 Project and repo
  FinVerse (startup + IIT Bombay ITSP 2026 project): a realistic simulated stock market where
  students trade against AI investors and learn the theory alongside. Engine: a fork of
  ABIDES. Repo ApratimAnand336/Abides_market_sim. Prices are integer CENTS, variances
  cents^2, engine time ns. Price about $1000 (r_bar = 100,000 c). 1 tick = 1 c = 0.1 bp.
  Read first: docs/HANDOFF.md, docs/engine/README.md, ARCHITECTURE.md, ORACLE.md,
  AGENT_FUNDAMENTALIST.md, AGENTS_ABIDES.md, REALISM_LAB.md, KNOWN_ISSUES.md,
  docs/v1.4/lab/RESULTS.md (R3 results).

0.2 The current default market "P0" (v1.4)
  60 informed FundamentalistAgents (design D-60fl2M: research every log-U[60,300] s with
  noise log-U[10,50] c, $2M each, belief-anchored prices, learning from prices off),
  12 MomentumEKFAgents, 1000 ABIDES NoiseAgents, 2 ABIDES AdaptiveMarketMakerAgents,
  gross budget L = 2 on the stock agents, no ValueAgents. True value: PregeneratedOracle,
  path source "synthetic_ou" (calm OU, 1.58 c per sqrt(s), about 1.2 bps per minute).

0.3 Where P0 stands (20 seeds, docs/v1.4/lab/RESULTS.md)
  PASS: |ac1| 0.005; VR(5) 1.2488 (by 0.001); volume-volatility +0.53; volume x2.5 after a
  +/-2% value jump; informed P&L +$1,023/run; spread 0.30/1.0 bps; 522k shares/day; 26 s/day.
  OPEN:
    K14 (T3) price too smooth at 1-5 min: price/value vol 0.49 at 1 min (0.90 at 60 min).
    K15 (T6) after a +/-2% jump the spread p95 is 14.8x / 16.0x baseline while informed
        traders disagree (the book never empties).
    K16 informed leverage bound exceeded by <= 0.9 pp of wealth right after jumps.
    G1/G2 volatility 0.62 bps/min, 0.20%/day (band 5-10 bps/min, 1-2%/day): the value
        process itself is too calm (about 1.2 bps/min).
    G5 clustering WARN; G6 kurtosis 1.47 (WARN); G11 regimes 2.5x; G12 impact beta 0.94.
  Engine speed: after the v1.4 OrderBook fix, order deepcopies (~15%) and debug-log
  formatting (~8%) lead the profile.

0.4 Why the ABIDES NoiseAgents are replaced (owner asked for a simple agent that fixes
    exactly their gaps)
  What they do: each of 1000 agents wakes ONCE per day (U-quadratic time 09:00-16:00),
  flips a coin for the side, sends one order at the opposite best price with a random size
  (about 100 shares typical), then does nothing more. They are the uninformed flow that pays
  the informed traders (7.5% of P0 volume, -$885/day).
  Gaps -> fixes in the new RetailTraderAgent crowd (Part A4):
    G-a never react to the market (no panic, no dip buying, no rush on news)
        -> activity rises with recent volatility and news (self-exciting crowd intensity)
    G-b one order per day, fixed rate                -> many small actions, intensity varies
    G-c hold no position, cannot use stop-losses     -> each trader holds shares and cash;
                                                        some use stop-losses (registry)
    G-d no behaviour types                           -> three simple types: liquidity,
                                                        trend-chaser, contrarian
    G-e stock ABIDES agent (owner: don't depend on them) -> our own agent
  Kept deliberately simple: no Kalman filters, no beliefs; a few rules and a shared crowd
  scheduler for speed.

0.5 Why our own market makers
  The ABIDES AdaptiveMarketMaker quotes a 10-level ladder around the mid every ~60 s with
  spacing tied to its own spread estimate (it needed a cap in v1.2 because of a runaway
  feedback), has no inventory management (skew off) and unlimited inventory (exempt from the
  budget). K15 needs liquidity that stays tight while informed traders disagree and that
  widens/withdraws sensibly under stress (also a crash ingredient in Part B).

0.6 Crash facts used in Part B (6 May 2010; Kirilenko, Kyle, Samadi, Tuzun, "The Flash
    Crash: High-Frequency Trading in an Electronic Market", CFTC; SEC-CFTC joint report)
  - Trigger: a fund's algorithm sold 75,000 E-mini contracts (about $4.1 bn) in about
    20 minutes, without regard to price, into an already stressed market; a previous sale of
    that size had taken more than 5 hours.
  - E-mini fell 5.1% in 13 minutes, then rose 6.4% in the next 23 minutes; back near pre-drop
    levels about 36 minutes after the start.
  - Volume per minute about 8x the earlier level; resting buy-side depth fell below 1% of its
    starting level at the bottom.
  - A 5-second CME stop-logic pause marked the bottom; prices stabilised after it.
  - Fundamental buyers bought heavily on the way up; market makers were "run over" in both
    directions and ended with small net inventory changes; HFT volume was large but their net
    position barely changed.
  Indian circuit breakers (for Part C): market-wide halts at index moves of 10/15/20%
  (45/105 min or rest of day depending on time); individual stocks in derivatives have no
  daily price band; others have 2/5/10/20% bands. US post-2010 pilot: a stock moving 10%
  in 5 minutes is paused for 5 minutes.

PART 2. GLOBAL RULES FOR THIS SPEC
==================================================================================
2.1 Pre-registration: every target, limit, selection rule and expectation below is fixed
    before any run. Results are graded mechanically. Nothing is tuned outside the
    pre-registered sweeps. A missed target is a finding, reported with evidence.
2.2 Inert defaults until the switch: every new mechanism is behind an argument whose default
    reproduces v1.4 P0 bit-for-bit (I8, the current P0 invariant hash) until milestone A9,
    which switches the default. v1.4 P0 must stay reproducible forever afterwards
    (I8-legacy-v14, new in A9) and the v1.3 market too (existing I8-legacy).
2.3 Determinism: new randomness only from dedicated RandomStates seeded from the config's
    seeded np.random, drawn AFTER all existing draws (or from the oracle's own RNG for
    everything that is part of the "world": day type, stress periods, news times, jump sizes,
    headline choices). Never change existing draw orders.
2.4 Test every new rule on every agent type and situation it touches (lesson of v1.4.1,
    where a rule tested only on uninformed agents hurt informed ones): each new behaviour
    has unit tests for quiet day, news, and large move, from the point of view of each agent
    class affected.
2.5 Units: state units on every number (cents, bps, seconds, shares, $).
2.6 Speed: L5 (median full day <= 90 s at 2 workers) remains a hard limit; the goal for the
    default market is <= 30 s per day (report).

A3. THE LIVING VALUE PROCESS (oracle path source "living_v1")  [milestone A3]
  A3.1 Model (cents, seconds; 1-s grid 09:30-16:00, N = 23,401 points, as v1.2 grid)
       v_{k+1} = r_bar + (v_k - r_bar) a + sigma_k * sqrt(1 s) * eps_k + J_k     (a, OU pull as
       synthetic_ou with kappa_oracle; negligible within a day)
       sigma_k = sigma_base * m_day * U(t_k) * S_k
         sigma_base = 7.1 c per sqrt(s)  (= 5.5 bps per minute at $1000; fund_vol 2.25e-4 c/sqrt(ns))
         m_day: calm 0.6, normal 1.0, volatile 1.6
         U(t) intraday shape, t in hours since 09:30:
             U(t) = (1 + 0.6 exp(-t/0.5) + 0.4 exp(-(6.5 - t)/0.5)) / c_U,
             c_U = mean of the numerator over the session grid (so mean U = 1)
         S_k stress state, 2-state Markov chain on the 1-s grid: S = 1 (normal) or 2.5
             (stressed). Entry probability per second p_in = e_day / (6.5 * 3600) with
             e_day (expected stress episodes per day) calm 0.2, normal 0.7, volatile 1.5;
             exit probability per second p_out = 1 / (45 * 60) (mean episode 45 min).
             Start in state 1.
       Information jumps J_k: number ~ Poisson(n_day), n_day calm 2, normal 3, volatile 4;
         times ~ Uniform(09:35, 15:55) (sorted); size |J| = r_bar * min(m, 0.02) with
         m ~ LogNormal(ln 0.0020, 0.6) (median 0.20% of price, cap 2%); sign 50/50.
       Megashocks: keep parameters but default rate 0 in living_v1.
  A3.2 Draw order from the symbol's dedicated RandomState (fixed, document it):
       1 day type (if "auto": u ~ U(0,1); calm if u < 0.30, normal if u < 0.80, else volatile)
       2 N uniforms for the stress chain
       3 N-1 normals eps
       4 news count, then times, then sizes, then signs, then headline template indices.
       The path stays a pure function of (seed, params, grid, session) (I2 must hold).
  A3.3 Expected statistics of the VALUE (verified by Claude with an independent numpy
       simulation of exactly this model, 200 days per type, 2026-10-16; T-O5 must reproduce
       them within +/-10% over 200 seeds):
         calm:     1-min sd 3.8 bps, daily move sd 0.78%
         normal:   1-min sd 6.3 bps, daily move sd 1.49%
         volatile: 1-min sd 11.3 bps, daily move sd 2.42%
         default mix 30/50/20: 1-min sd median 6.2 bps, daily sd 1.53%
         per-day 30-min regime contrast of the value itself: median 2.4-2.9 (p90 3.8-4.4)
  A3.4 Information news: for each jump, the NewsOracleAgent publishes a headline AT the jump
       time (public news). Headline from docs/engine/news_templates.json (new): >= 60
       templates, balanced: {positive, negative} x {small (< 0.3%), medium (0.3-1%), large
       (> 1%)}, each with a stored FinBERT sentiment (computed once by
       scripts/score_templates.py and committed; runs never call FinBERT). The template is
       drawn uniformly within the bucket matching the jump sign and size. A template whose
       stored sentiment sign disagrees with its bucket is a build error (test).
       Agents react to headlines exactly as today (fundamentalist belief shift, momentum
       priming) plus the retail crowd (A4). The informed agents learn the jump SIZE through
       research (1-5 min), as before.
  A3.5 Config: build_config(value_process="synthetic_ou" | "living_v1", day_type="auto" |
       "calm" | "normal" | "volatile", auto_news=True|False). Defaults keep v1.4 until A9.

A4. RETAIL TRADER CROWD (replaces ABIDES NoiseAgents)  [milestone A4]
  A4.1 Two classes (new files abides-markets/abides_markets/agents/retail.py):
       RetailCrowdAgent (exactly one): every dt_c = 5 s it
         (1) reads the L1 book (bid, ask; via its own spread query every 5 s) and keeps a
             ring buffer of 5-s mids (last 60 min);
         (2) computes the crowd intensity
               lambda(t) = mu * U_act(t) * min(10, 1 + kappa_v * max(0, V(t) - 1)
                                                   + kappa_n * Nn(t))   (actions per second)
             U_act(t) = U(t) of A3.1 (same shape: busier at open and close).
             V(t) = sigma_short / sigma_ref: sigma_short^2 = mean over the last 24 5-s
               intervals of (ln mid_i - ln mid_{i-1})^2 / 5 s; sigma_ref^2 = EWMA of the same
               per-second variance with half-life 60 min (V = 1 during the first 10 min).
             Nn(t): +|sentiment| on each NewsSentimentMsg, decays with half-life 10 min.
         (3) draws n ~ Poisson(lambda * dt_c) and for each action picks a RetailTraderAgent
             uniformly at random and sends it an ActNow message (with the current snapshot:
             bid, ask, mid, r_5 = ln(mid/mid_5min_ago), r_30 = ln(mid/mid_30min_ago),
             s_30 = typical 30-min sd = sqrt(sigma_ref^2 * 1800)).
         (4) STOP REGISTRY: keeps every retail trader's stop price; if mid <= a stop it sends
             that trader a StopTriggered message (once) and removes the stop.
       RetailTraderAgent (N_r agents): no wakeups of its own. Holds cash and shares
         (endowment: wealth $50,000, 50% in stock at r_bar = 25 shares). Long-only and
         cash-only (no shorting, no borrowing). Type drawn at construction:
           liquidity 50%: side Bernoulli(0.5)
           trend 25%:     P(buy) = 1 / (1 + exp(-beta * r_30 / s_30)), beta = 0.87
                          (P(buy) = 0.85 after a +2 sd 30-min rise; 0.15 after a -2 sd fall)
           contrarian 25%: P(buy) = 1 / (1 + exp(+beta * r_30 / s_30))
         Stop users: 40% of agents (any type). After each buy fill they register
           stop = fill price * (1 - d_i), d_i ~ U[0.02, 0.06] (per agent, fixed); on
           StopTriggered they sell all shares.
         Order on ActNow: size q ~ round(LogNormal(ln 30, 0.8)) shares, at least 1, clipped by
           cash (buy) or shares held (sell); a sell with 0 shares or a buy with no cash is
           skipped (logged). Marketable limit order with a 2% broker collar (retail brokers
           protect market orders this way): buy limit = ask * 1.02, sell limit = bid * 0.98,
           rounded to cents (buy up, sell down); it fills against the book up to the collar
           and any remainder rests at the collar price. At every ActNow or StopTriggered the trader
           first cancels all its own open orders, so a remainder lives only until its next action. On StopTriggered: sell all at bid * (1 - 0.02).
  A4.2 Parameters (param_ranges.py RETAIL): mu = mu_per_agent * N_r with mu_per_agent =
       4 actions per trader per day (spread over 23,400 s); kappa_n = 2.0 fixed;
       kappa_v and N_r are sweep dials (A7).
  A4.3 Expected crowd behaviour (verified by Claude with a numpy simulation of A4.1 (2),
       mu = 2000 actions/day, 2026-10-16): quiet day about 1,800 actions, open-to-midday
       activity ratio 1.6; +5 min after a 0.9 headline about 2.3x midday activity; during a
       shock with V = 6, about 8x (kappa_v = 1) to 13x (kappa_v = 3), back to about 1x within
       30 min of the shock ending.
  A4.4 Tests (abides-markets/tests/test_retail.py), each agent type covered (rule 2.4):
       T-RT1 crowd intensity formula, cap 10, V warm-up, news decay (1e-9 exact on a hand
             series).
       T-RT2 types: for r_30/s_30 in {-2, 0, +2}: trend P(buy) = 0.15/0.5/0.85, contrarian
             0.85/0.5/0.15, liquidity 0.5 (+/-0.01 over 20,000 draws, fixed seed).
       T-RT3 stops: a buy fill registers the stop; mid crossing it triggers exactly one
             full sell; no stop for non-stop users; stops survive across actions.
       T-RT4 budget: never negative cash or shares at any fill (I4 extended).
       T-RT5 one-hour market with the crowd instead of NoiseAgents trades all hour; the crowd
             places >= 200 orders; P&L zero_sum = 0.
       T-RT6 determinism: two runs identical (I1).
  A4.5 Config: build_config(num_noise=..., num_retail=0 (default until A9), retail_kappa_v).
       Measured alone (campaign a-retail, 10 seeds, synthetic_ou value): P0 with NoiseAgents

A5. OUR MARKET MAKERS (replace the ABIDES market makers)  [milestone A5]
  A5.1 New agent LiquidityProviderAgent (abides-markets/abides_markets/agents/
       liquidity_provider.py). Wakes every 5 s (fixed, offset by agent index to avoid
       collisions). State: inventory q (shares), cash, wealth W, sigma_hat.
       On wake: query the spread (L1). Then:
         ref = mid if both sides exist, else last trade price, else cold start r_bar.
         sigma_hat^2 = EWMA (half-life 5 min) of (ln ref_t - ln ref_{t-5s})^2 / 5 s
           (per-second variance; prior = (5.5 bps/min)^2 / 60 s).
         sigma_10 = ref * sqrt(sigma_hat^2 * 10 s)          (typical 10-s move, cents)
         h = max(1 c, c_h * sigma_10)                         half-spread, c_h = 0.5
         q_max = floor(l_max * W / ref), l_max = 0.5           inventory limit (shares)
         skew = psi * (q / q_max) * h, psi = 1.0                (long inventory -> lower quotes)
         reservation r = ref - skew
         Ladder: 3 levels per side at r -/+ h * (1, 2, 3); size per level
           s_lvl = max(1, floor(theta_lp * W / ref / 3)), theta_lp = 0.02 (2% of wealth per
           side in total).
         Inventory limits: no bids if q >= q_max; no asks if q <= -q_max. If |q| > 1.2 q_max
           (overshoot by fills), send a marketable order to bring |q| back to q_max (collar 2%).
         Lazy requote (speed): keep the existing quotes unless |ref - ref_quoted| > h/2, or
           |q - q_quoted| > 0.1 q_max, or 30 s have passed, or a side is missing; otherwise do
           nothing this wake (no cancel, no new orders).
         Budget: gross position value <= 2 W (as stock agents); MMs may short within q_max.
  A5.2 Why these rules: quoting around the current price with a width tied to recent
       volatility keeps liquidity between disagreeing informed traders (K15); inventory skew
       and limits make the MM lean against flow and withdraw a side when full (realistic, and
       the crash ingredient in Part B); lazy requotes keep the engine fast.
  A5.3 Parameters: count n_lp and capital W_lp are sweep dials (A7); c_h, psi, l_max,
       theta_lp fixed as above (param_ranges.py LP; documented).
  A5.4 Tests (test_liquidity_provider.py; rule 2.4: quiet book, one-sided book, empty book,
       inventory at limit, fast move):
       T-LP1 quote prices/sizes exactly as A5.1 for given (ref, sigma_hat, q, W).
       T-LP2 inventory limit: at q = q_max no bid is placed; at -q_max no ask.
       T-LP3 lazy requote: no orders are sent when ref and q are unchanged within thresholds;
             a move > h/2 triggers a requote.
       T-LP4 cold start: empty book -> quotes around r_bar; one-sided book -> around last trade.
       T-LP5 sigma_hat rises after a fast move -> h widens (monotone).
       T-LP6 budget and zero_sum in a 1-hour market; invariants I1-I9.
  A5.5 Config: build_config(mm_kind="abides" (default until A9) | "lp", num_lp, lp_wealth).
       Measured alone (campaign a-lp, 10 seeds, synthetic_ou): P0 with ABIDES MMs replaced by
       2 LPs of $5M. Report vs P0 (especially T6 and spread).

A6. K16 FIX AND SMALL ITEMS  [milestone A6]
  A6.1 K16 (informed budget exceeded by <= 0.9 pp after jumps). First diagnose with a
       targeted test reproducing it (P0, S2, seed with the largest excess). Fix the root
       cause so that the informed bound holds at EVERY fill with a tolerance of one share:
       the budget check must use the worst-case price at which the order can fill (its
       limit price) for both the position and the wealth, and must count every own order
       that can still fill (resting, or cancelled but not yet confirmed). Tighten I4 for
       informed agents to that tolerance. Behaviour changes are expected to be tiny; they
       are behind the default switch (A9) like everything else (I8 until then: implement the
       fix behind informed_budget_v2=True|False, default False until A9).
  A6.2 Re-add IMP-0.1 to the impact set (beta on four sizes).
  A6.3 T2 margin (VR(5) 1.2488 in v1.4): no action; report VR(5) prominently.
  A6.4 Regime band (G11). The A3.3 simulation shows that even the true value itself has a
       median per-day 30-min regime contrast of only 2.4-2.9 under realistic day types; one
       day of 13 half-hours mostly measures the intraday U-shape and sampling noise. G11
       (per day >= 5) is therefore unreachable by construction and is moved to REPORTED.
       Regimes are graded ACROSS days instead (A8 target RS). This is a band correction made
       before any R4 run, from the model's own maths; record it in DECISIONS.md.

A7. DESIGN SWEEP (pre-registered)  [milestone A7]

PART B. CRASHES (R4b)

B1. PRINCIPLE
  A crash is never drawn. A scenario sets (1) a TRIGGER and (2) the market's FRAGILITY; the
  path comes from the agents. Scenarios are off by default and selected explicitly
  (RunParams.scenario; dashboard selector "Stress scenario"). Each scenario on different
  seeds gives a different but similar crash.

B2. NEW MECHANISMS  [milestone B1]
  B2.1 ForcedSellerAgent (forced_seller.py): a price-insensitive sell program modelled on the
       6 May 2010 algorithm (target a fixed share of recent volume, regardless of price).
       Parameters: start time t_s, total quantity Q (shares), participation rho = 0.09.
       Every 10 s from t_s: sell min(remaining, max(1, round(rho * V_60))) shares, V_60 =
       shares executed in the market over the previous 60 s (from its own trade-volume
       query), as a marketable sell at bid * (1 - 0.05) (wide collar: price-insensitive).
       Stops when Q is sold. Starts with Q shares and enough cash (exempt from the gross
       budget, like the impact probe; included in zero_sum). Logs every child and a summary.
       Feedback by design: more volume -> faster selling (as on 6 May).
  B2.2 Margin calls (default ON from B4; behind margin_calls=True|False until then):
       informed agents: maintenance bounds w_long_maint = 1.75, w_short_maint = -0.75 of
       current wealth (at mid). On any wake or fill, if w > 1.75, send a marketable sell
       (collar 2%) to bring w to 1.5; if w < -0.75, a marketable buy to -0.5. Logged
       MARGIN_CALL. Retail: cash-only, no margin. LPs: A5.1 inventory overshoot rule already
       unwinds. Momentum EKF: long-only, unchanged.
  B2.3 Fragility profile ("stressed"): applied only by crash scenarios:
       retail stop users 40% -> 60%; LP l_max 0.5 -> 0.25 (smaller inventory tolerance);
       momentum capital x5 (mom_capital_mult = 5); everything else unchanged.
  B2.4 Tests (rule 2.4: each agent class in a falling and a rising market):
       T-FS1 child sizes follow rho * V_60 exactly; stops at Q; never buys.
       T-FS2 zero_sum = 0 with the seller present.
       T-MC1 margin call fires exactly when w crosses the maintenance bound and restores the
             bound; no margin call in a market without large moves (normal day, 3 seeds).
       T-MC2 a margin call never pushes w past the opposite bound.

B3. SCENARIOS  [milestone B2]
  All on a NORMAL day (day_type "normal"), event at 13:00 (offset 03:30:00), fragility
  "stressed", default design otherwise. Severities:
    CR-FLASH-M / -S   liquidity crash: ForcedSeller with Q = 5% / 15% of the design's median
                      daily volume (from A9.1 N-C), no value change, no headline.
    CR-INFO-M / -S    information crash: value jump of -4% / -8% at 13:00 with a negative
                      headline from a "crash" template bucket (sentiment <= -0.8).
    CR-RUMOR-M / -S   rumour: a negative headline (sentiment -0.8 / -0.95) with no value
                      change, plus a ForcedSeller of Q = 2% / 5% (panic selling by one fund
                      that believed it).
  Each: 10 seeds; baseline = the same seed's normal day with fragility "stressed" and no
  trigger (configs CR-BASE). 6 x 10 + 10 = 70 runs.

B4. CRASH SIGNATURES (pre-registered; median over 10 seeds; window W = [t_s, t_s + 90 min])
  Define drop D = (pre-crash price - minimum mid in W) / pre-crash price, where pre-crash price
  = mean mid over [t_s - 5 min, t_s]; trough time t_min; t_down = t_min - t_s.
  Common to every SEVERE scenario:
    C1 a real crash: D >= 3% (FLASH-S, RUMOR-S) and D >= 8% (INFO-S, at least the value move)
    C2 liquidity evaporates: top-10-level buy-side depth at the trough <= 25% of its median
       over the pre-crash hour (real: < 1%)
    C3 spread blows out: p95 spread in [t_s, t_min + 5 min] >= 5x the pre-crash-hour p95
    C4 volume spikes: peak 1-min volume in W >= 4x the pre-crash-hour median (real: ~8x)
    C5 volatility persists: sigma_1m over [t_min + 30, t_min + 90 min] >= 1.5x pre-crash hour
    C6 accounting and liveness: zero_sum = 0, invariants pass, no price <= 0, every LP wakes
  Shape by kind:
    C7 FLASH-S: recovery R60 = (mid(t_min + 60 min) - min) / (pre - min) >= 0.6 (real: about
       1 within 36 min). Report t_down and the time from t_min to 50% recovery (6 May: down
       13 min, back up 23 min); their order is not graded.
    C8 INFO-S: overshoot: min mid <= new value - 0.5% of price; settle: |mid(t_s + 90 min) -
       new value| <= 1% of price; no full recovery (mid(t_s + 90) <= pre - 5%).
    C9 RUMOR-S: recovery R60 >= 0.5 and |mid(t_s + 90 min) - value| <= 1% of price.
    C10 who traded (qualitative, reported with numbers; pass if all hold): on the down leg
       [t_s, t_min] the ForcedSeller (if any), stop-loss sells and momentum agents are net
       sellers; on the up leg [t_min, t_min + 60 min] informed fundamentalists are net
       buyers; LPs' |net inventory change over W| <= 20% of their gross volume in W (they
       intermediate, like 6 May market makers).
  MILD scenarios: report all measures; C1 thresholds halved (D >= 1.5% / 4%); C2-C5 reported
  only (mild stress need not empty the book).
  Default-day guarantee (D-R4-6): over the 20 D-mix confirmation runs of A9.1 no day has
  D >= 3% within any 30-min window without a scenario (report the max).

PART C. CIRCUIT BREAKER (optional, OFF by default; after Parts A and B)  [milestones C1-C2]
==================================================================================
C1. PURPOSE (owner asked): real exchanges pause trading when prices move too fast, to give
    traders time to think and liquidity time to return (the 5-second CME pause on 6 May;
    the US 10%-in-5-minutes pause; Indian market-wide halts). In FinVerse it lets a student
    compare the same crash with and without a pause.
C2. MECHANISM (ExchangeAgent extension, behind circuit_breaker=None | dict):
    rule: if |mid(t) - mid(t - 5 min)| / mid(t - 5 min) >= theta (default 0.10; scenario
    parameter), halt for H = 300 s. During a halt the exchange matches nothing: new limit
    orders are queued, cancels are honoured, market orders are rejected (logged). At the end
    of the halt a single-price reopening auction uncrosses the queued book: the price that
    maximises executed volume (ties: minimum imbalance, then closest to the last trade
    price); orders crossing that price execute at it (price-time priority within a side);
    the rest rest in the book. Log HALT_START, HALT_END, AUCTION {price, volume}.
    At most one halt per 30 min. Agents receive a MarketHaltMsg / MarketResumeMsg; agents that
    poll during a halt see the queued book (no trades).
C3. TESTS: T-CB1 auction price on hand-built books (known answers); T-CB2 no matching during a
    halt; T-CB3 zero_sum and invariants with halts; T-CB4 off by default (I8 unchanged).
C4. CAMPAIGN: CR-FLASH-S and CR-INFO-S with the breaker (theta 0.05 and 0.10), 10 seeds each
    (40 runs); compare with Part B's runs: D, depth at the trough, recovery R60, volume.
    Reported only (no targets): this is a teaching feature.

APPENDIX A. FORMULA SHEET
==================================================================================
  Value (living_v1):  dv = sigma_base * m_day * U(t) * S(t) dW + jumps
     sigma_base 7.1 c/sqrt(s); m_day 0.6/1.0/1.6; S in {1, 2.5}, p_in = e_day/(6.5 h),
     mean stress 45 min; e_day 0.2/0.7/1.5; jumps Poisson 2/3/4 per day, |J| = r_bar *
     min(LogNormal(ln 0.002, 0.6), 0.02)
  Crowd:  lambda = mu * U(t) * min(10, 1 + kappa_v * max(0, V - 1) + 2 * Nn)
     V = sigma_short / sigma_ref (2-min vs 60-min half-life), Nn += |s| per headline, half-life 10 min
     trend P(buy) = sigma(0.87 * r30/s30); contrarian P(buy) = sigma(-0.87 * r30/s30)
     size LogNormal(ln 30, 0.8); collar 2%; stops at fill * (1 - U[2%, 6%]) for 40% of traders
  LP:  ref; sigma_10 = ref * sqrt(sigma_hat^2 * 10); h = max(1 c, 0.5 * sigma_10)
     q_max = 0.5 W/ref; r = ref - (q/q_max) h; 3 levels at r -/+ k h; 2% of W per side
     lazy requote: |ref - ref_q| > h/2 or |q - q_q| > 0.1 q_max or 30 s
  Forced seller: every 10 s sell max(1, round(0.09 * V_60)) until Q; collar 5%
  Margin call: informed w > 1.75 -> sell to 1.5; w < -0.75 -> buy to -0.5
  Circuit breaker: |mid_t / mid_{t-5min} - 1| >= theta -> 300 s halt + max-volume auction
