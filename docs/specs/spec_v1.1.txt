0.1 What FinVerse is
  FinVerse is a startup and IIT Bombay ITSP 2026 project: a realistic simulated stock market
  where students practise trading against AI investors and learn theory alongside. The
  engine is a fork of ABIDES (J.P. Morgan's discrete-event market simulator,
  arXiv:1904.12066). We add our own agent types (a Kalman-filter "fundamentalist" and a
  Kalman-filter "momentum" investor), a news system, and a web dashboard.

0.3 The simulated market (rmsc04_ekf.py, defaults)
  Price around $1000 (r_bar = 100,000 cents). ABIDES prices are integer CENTS; variances
  are in cents^2. Every agent starts with $100,000 (starting_cash = 10,000,000 cents).
    1000 NoiseAgent        about one order each per day
      97 ValueAgent        each looks at the hidden true value (the "oracle") every ~175 s
                           with noise variance 1000 cents^2; buys if estimate >= mid, else sells;
                           orders are about 106 shares on average; no cash checks
       2 AdaptiveMarketMakerAgent
      12 momentum slots    num_mom_ekf_agents of them are our MomentumEKFAgent; the rest are
                           the ABIDES MA momentum agent
    num_ekf_agents FundamentalistAgent (ours; the dashboard allows 0-20)
       1 ExchangeAgent, 1 NewsOracleAgent
  Our agents start with an endowment: initial_exposure 0.5, so 50 shares + $50,000 cash.
  The oracle is a SparseMeanRevertingOracle. NOTE: observe_price(sigma_n=...) treats
  sigma_n as a VARIANCE (cents^2), not a standard deviation.

0.4 Measured facts (seed 42, one full day 09:30-16:00) used for calibration below
  - about 30,000 trades/day; median spread 1 cent
  - oracle change std: 10 s 4.9 cents; 1 min 13 cents; 5 min 32 cents; 30 min 82 cents.
    These grow like sqrt(time), so the oracle is close to a random walk with
    sigma_f^2 of about 3 cents^2 per second. Day range $996.54-$1000.71.
  - mid minus oracle gap: std 37 cents; autocorrelation 0.99 (10 s), 0.92 (1 min),
    0.63 (5 min), 0.29 (10 min); half-life of a mispricing about 7.5 minutes
  - mid wiggle around its 1-minute average: about 2 cents
  - Kaufman ER of the mid (10 s samples, window 10): median 0.48 (10th-90th: 0.08-0.92)

0.5 Problems this spec fixes (diagnosed on seed 42, 20 fundamentalists, 12 momentum, news
    "Company announces record quarterly earnings" +0.9 at 00:10:00)
  Fundamentalist:
    (a) After every order, x_hat *= 1.01 or 0.99 (a "step 9" heuristic, origin unknown).
        That is a $10 jump per order, so x_hat random-walked to about $949.
    (b) No process noise Q: P only shrinks, the gain K -> 1/(n + R/P0), K about 0.0004 by the
        close. The belief freezes into an all-day average and cannot correct (a).
    (c) R = (delta*price)^2 * exp(lambda_er*(1-ER)) with delta in [0.002, 0.015] and
        lambda_er in [1, 6]. The agent believes prices are $2.6-$70 off when the real noise is
        cents to tens of cents.
    (d) Sizing: each wake trades a fraction of current holdings and at least 1 share whenever
        x_hat != mid. 19 of 20 sold the whole 50-share endowment 7-19 minutes after the open.
    (e) News multiplies x_hat by (1 + news_sensitivity*sentiment): +$5-$40 (+$33 in the test).
  Momentum EKF:
    (f) The trend speed is underestimated. Steady-state ramp analysis:
          v_hat/v = dt / ((1-phi)(tau + K_l/K_v))  ~=  tau / (tau + K_l/K_v)
        (checked exactly against a direct simulation). With kappa_l = 0.5 and
        kappa_v = 0.5 the agent sees only 26-28% of the true speed.
    (g) The realised volatility uses raw returns, so trend drift counts as volatility
        (E[r^2/dt] = sigma^2 + v^2*dt). Exposure scale g fell to 0.43 in clean trends
        (0.81 detrended).
    (h) tau is drawn independently of wake_mean. When tau/wake <= 5, |T| >= 2 was reached
        0% of the time.
    (i) The ratchet cap only rises, so the agent re-buys without a new high.
    (j) Re-entry needs only v_hat > 0. 6 of 9 re-entries reversed within 3 looks
        (0 of 9 after the fix in replay).
    (k) A STOP exit is urgent only on the wake it fires. If that order is skipped or unfilled,
        the urgency is lost.
  Dashboard:
    (l) Belief lines clipped to the price axis draw vertical streaks.
    (m) P&L includes the mark-to-market of the endowment; there is no "vs just holding" view.
    (n) Momentum level lines for all agents clutter the main chart.
    (o) There is no view of the rest of the market population (value/noise/MM counts, flow).
    (p) There is no readout of how tightly the price hugs true value.

PART 1. NOTATION AND UNITS
=====================================================================
  mid      (bid+ask)/2 in cents
  x_hat    fundamentalist belief of value (cents);  P its variance (cents^2)
  Q        process-noise variance added at each prediction (cents^2)
  R        observation-noise variance of the mid reading (cents^2)
  K        Kalman gain = P_pred/(P_pred + R)
  dt       seconds since the agent's previous Kalman update (from time_log, in ns -> s)
  C_t      caution/confidence from the existing sigmoid (unchanged), in [0, 1]
  W        wealth = cash + shares*mid (cents)
  All new hyperparameters are drawn through param_ranges.draw(); add them to the ranges dicts
  so the dashboard shows them.

PART 2. FUNDAMENTALIST AGENT (fundamental.py)
=====================================================================
Leave unchanged: the lifecycle, wakeup/receive_message flow, Kaufman ER (_compute_kaufman_er),
the caution modulator (update_caution, gamma, k), the oracle-based initial estimate
(sigma_n ranges unchanged), the endowment, and the decision_limit_price formula apart from
the side change in F4.4.

F1. Delete the nudge (step 9)
  Remove the whole "9. Slow-moving Fundamental Equation Update" block
  (x_hat *= 1.01 / 0.99). After this, x_hat changes ONLY in correction() and on news (F5).
  Update the module docstring accordingly.

F2. Observation variance R (smaller delta, gentler ER penalty)
  Formula unchanged:  R_t = (delta * mid)^2 * exp(lambda_er * (1 - ER_t))
  New ranges in FUNDAMENTAL_RANGES:
      delta      (0.0005, 0.003, "linear")   was (0.002, 0.015)   -> delta*mid = $0.50-$3
      lambda_er  (0.5, 2.0, "linear")        was (1.0, 6.0)       -> multiplier 1.3x-2.7x at median ER
  Update the constructor defaults to delta = 0.0015, lambda_er = 1.0.
  Why this size: readings 10 s apart are strongly correlated (the gap autocorrelation is 0.92
  at 1 min), so the effective per-reading noise is about 30x the instantaneous gap variance:
  sqrt(30 * 0.37^2) = about $2. The new range brackets that.

F3. Process noise Q (new)
  prediction(dt) replaces prediction():
      x_pred = x_hat
      P_pred = P + Q_t,      Q_t = q * sigma_f2_hat * dt
  q: personality, new range "q": (0.3, 3.0, "log"). Store it as self.q.
  dt: seconds between this update's time and the previous update's time (time_log). The
      first update after initialisation uses its true dt. News-triggered extra cycles have a
      short dt; that is correct.
  sigma_f2_hat (cents^2 per second): the agent's own estimate of how fast value moves,
      measured from LONG-lag mid changes, because short-lag wiggles are noise:
      constants (module-level, not randomised):
          SIGMA_F_LAG_S   = 300.0     lag in seconds
          SIGMA_F_PRIOR   = 3.0       cents^2/s, calibrated from 0.4
          SIGMA_F_FLOOR   = 0.1       cents^2/s
          SIGMA_F_EWMA_N  = 60        EWMA memory in updates (alpha = 1/60)
      initialise self.sigma_f2_hat = SIGMA_F_PRIOR
      at each update (after appending mid to price_log/time_log):
          find the most recent log index j with time_log[j] <= now - SIGMA_F_LAG_S
          if it exists:
              lag_s  = (now - time_log[j]) in seconds
              sample = (mid - price_log[j])^2 / lag_s
              sigma_f2_hat = (1 - alpha)*sigma_f2_hat + alpha*sample
              sigma_f2_hat = max(sigma_f2_hat, SIGMA_F_FLOOR)
      (Use bisect on time_log; do not scan the whole list.)
  Expected behaviour: the gain settles at K_ss ~= sqrt(Q/R) instead of decaying to zero.
      With the new ranges, Q is about 9-90 cents^2 per 10 s and R about (50-300 cents)^2 x 1-2.7,
      so K_ss is about 0.006-0.19, a memory of about 1-30 minutes. sqrt(P) settles around
      tens of cents. The belief keeps up with the market at every hour of the day.
  Log: add "Q", "dt", "sigma_f2" to the EKF_UPDATE event (keep the existing keys).

F4. Order sizing: a target position (replaces decision_volume)
  Idea: decide how much to HOLD given the edge, then trade only the difference.
  Edge in units of the agent's own uncertainty, using the posterior P after correction:
      z = (x_hat - mid) / sqrt(P)
  Target fraction of wealth in the stock:
      w_star = clip( w0 + a_max * C_t * tanh(z / z0) , 0 , 1 )
          w0 = self.initial_exposure (0.5)
          new ranges:  "a_max": (0.6, 1.0, "linear")
                       "z0":    (1.0, 3.0, "linear")
  Desired change in shares:
      W       = cash + shares*mid
      d_star  = round( (w_star*W - shares*mid) / mid )
      step cap: |d| <= floor(s_max * W / mid),           "s_max": (0.10, 0.25, "linear")
      dead band: trade only if |d|*mid >= b_f * W and |d| >= 1,   "b_f": (0.01, 0.03, "linear")
      long-only and no leverage: sells <= shares held; buys <= floor(cash / limit_price)
  F4.4 Side and price: side = BUY if d > 0, SELL if d < 0. The side comes from d, not from the
      sign of (x_hat - mid). For example, after buying to 90% a small positive z can still
      target 60%, which is a SELL (taking profit). Change decision_limit_price to take the side
      explicitly; keep its formula per side (buy: bid + C*spread capped at x_hat*(1+mu),
      floored at bid; sell: ask - C*spread floored at x_hat*(1-mu), capped at ask). Remove its
      min_gap check, since the dead band replaces it.
  D_prev for the caution modulator stays the sign of (x_hat - mid), unchanged.
  Remove the beta draw inside the agent (self.beta) and remove "beta" from
  FUNDAMENTAL_RANGES. This changes the random stream; acceptable because the engine version
  is bumped.
  Log: new event "FUND_DECISION" each wake with z, w_star, w_now, d_star, d (after caps),
  skipped_reason ("dead_band" | "no_cash" | "no_shares" | null), side, limit_price.

F5. News: a volatility-scaled shift that also makes the agent more open-minded
  Replace the multiplicative shift in receive_message:
      sigma_move = sqrt(sigma_f2_hat * NEWS_HORIZON_S)        NEWS_HORIZON_S = 1800.0 (module constant)
      shift      = sentiment * k_news * sigma_move              (cents, signed)
      x_hat      = x_hat + shift
      P          = P + shift^2
  New range "k_news": (0.5, 3.0, "linear"). This replaces news_sensitivity: remove the
  parameter, its range and its UI entry.
  Example: sigma_f2 = 3 gives sigma_move = 73 cents; sentiment 0.9 and k_news 2 give
  +$1.32 (the old rule gave +$33).
  Intuition: news is worth k_news "typical 30-minute moves". The added P makes the next few
  market readings count more, so the market confirms or fades the news. If the market does
  not follow, the belief drifts back over the agent's memory.
  Keep the forced immediate trading cycle after news.
  NEWS_RECEIVED log keys become: headline, sentiment, shift (cents, signed), sigma_move,
  x_hat_before, x_hat_after, P_before, P_after.
  (The old "shift" key was a multiplier; see D-export changes in 4.2.)

F6. Config (rmsc04_ekf.py)
  Draw the new and changed fundamentalist hyperparameters via draw(FUND, ...): delta,
  lambda_er, q, a_max, z0, s_max, b_f, k_news. Remove beta and news_sensitivity.
  sigma_n, initial_uncertainty, gamma, k, mu, er_window are unchanged.

PART 3. MOMENTUM EKF AGENT (momentum_ekf.py), changes to spec v1.0
=====================================================================
M1. Narrower level/velocity noise ranges (fixes 0.5f)
  MOMENTUM_RANGES: "kappa_l": (0.02, 0.15, "linear")   was (0.05, 0.5)
                   "kappa_v": (1.0, 2.0, "linear")     was (0.5, 2.0)
  Meaning: kappa_l sets how plausible a random level jump is, kappa_v how plausible speed is.
  The fraction of a steady trend the agent sees is about tau/(tau + K_l/K_v); these ranges
  move it from as low as 26% to about 68-92%.

M2. Detrended volatility (fixes 0.5g)
  Everywhere a variance of returns is computed (warmup_fit -> sigma_ref2, and realised_var ->
  sigma_hat2, which also feeds sigma_tau and g), subtract the window's average drift first:
      r_i = z_i - z_{i-1},  dt_i = t_i - t_{i-1}       (z = log price, as now)
      v_bar = sum(r_i) / sum(dt_i)                      (time-weighted mean drift)
      e_i = r_i - v_bar * dt_i
      variance = mean( e_i^2 / dt_i )
  Keep the existing floors (sigma_q2 / mean(dt)). Keep the function names and signatures;
  add a docstring line citing this spec.

M3. Tie tau to the wake time (fixes 0.5h)
  Replace the "tau" range with "tau_ratio": (15.0, 40.0, "log"). In the config draw
  wake_mean FIRST, then tau = tau_ratio * wake_mean. The agent constructor still receives tau
  (keep the parameter) and also stores tau_ratio for display. wake_mean stays (10, 60, "linear").
  Result: 15-40 looks per trend lifetime; tau spans 2.5 to 40 minutes.

M4. The ratchet cap follows down (fixes 0.5i)
  ratchet_bull: first e_cap = min(e_cap, e_star); then, if p >= p_add + k_add*sigma_tau:
                e_cap = max(e_cap, e_star), p_add = p, added = True.
                Return (min(e_star, e_cap), e_cap, p_add, added).
  ratchet_bear (mirror): first e_floor = max(e_floor, e_star); then, if
                p <= p_add - k_add*sigma_tau: e_floor = min(e_floor, e_star), p_add = p,
                added = True. Return (max(e_star, e_floor), e_floor, p_add, added).
  Effect: exposure can move away from the trend freely, but moves toward it only at a fresh
  high (bull) or low (bear), including after a dip.

M5. Re-entry needs evidence (fixes 0.5j)
  In _step_state_machine, state WATCH:
      bull re-entry: p >= L + k_re*sigma_tau  AND  v_hat > 0  AND  T >= theta_out
      bear re-entry: p <= L - k_re*sigma_tau  AND  v_hat < 0  AND  T <= -theta_out

M6. Persistent stop exit (fixes 0.5k)
  New attribute self.exit_pending: bool = False.
  Set it to True when a STOP transition fires.
  While exit_pending is True, _decide_order uses order_class = STOP (urgent pricing) and does
  not skip for the dead band.
  Clear it when |current shares - target shares| < n_min, or on any entry (BREAKOUT/REENTRY).

M7. Logging and housekeeping
  MOM_DECISION adds: "added" (ratchet flag), "exit_pending", "sigma_hat2", "sigma_tau".
  Add "tau_ratio" to the exported hyperparameters.
  .gitignore: add  log/  and  *.pkl  (do not untrack or delete existing files).

PART 5. DEFERRED (do NOT implement in v1.1)
=====================================================================
  - Skewed initial estimate noise (80/20 log-normal mixture, overconfidence, persistent bias).
    Current initial error is only 17-55 cents std, so this is not needed now.
  - Self-calibrating R from innovations (R = kappa_R * s2_t * ...). F2's smaller delta is
    used instead. Revisit when value agents are replaced (the gap will grow and a fixed delta
    will need retuning).
  - Safety margin in uncertainty units (buy only below x_hat - m*sqrt(P)). The mu cap stays as
    is; F4's dead band removes the churn it was meant to stop.
  - Replacing ABIDES value agents with our fundamentalists, plus a periodic noisy "research"
    reading of the oracle as a second Kalman measurement. The oracle stays as hidden true
    value. Calibration targets for later: gap std $1-$3, half-life 30-90 min.
  - Two news types: "information" news moves the oracle; "hype" news moves beliefs only.
  - A herding tilt for the fundamentalist (temporary, used only in decisions).

=====================================================================
APPENDIX A. Why these maths (short)
=====================================================================
A1 Without Q:  P_n = 1/(1/P_0 + n/R),  K_n = 1/(n + 1 + R/P_0)  -> an all-day running average.
   With Q:     P_pred_ss = (Q + sqrt(Q^2 + 4QR))/2 ~= sqrt(QR),  K_ss ~= sqrt(Q/R),
               memory ~= 1/K_ss looks.
A2 Nudge equivalence: moving x_hat by 1% ($10) through the filter would need a surprise of
   $10/K. With K = 0.003 that is about $3,300, a phantom price.
A3 Ramp bias (momentum): at steady state the surprise y* is constant, so
       (1-phi)*v_hat = K_v*y*        and        v*dt = tau*(1-phi)*v_hat + K_l*y*
   which gives  v_hat/v = dt / ((1-phi)(tau + K_l/K_v)).
A4 Trend inside volatility: E[r^2/dt] = sigma^2 + v^2*dt; detrending removes the v^2*dt term.
A5 Mispricing half-life: treat the gap as a spring dg = -k*g*dt + noise; rho(L) = exp(-k*L),
   half-life = ln2/k.
